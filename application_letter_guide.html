Writing Task Guide

Letter of Application Guide

Вакансія: Part-time assistant at Day Camp Global, Cardiff Bay

Вимоги до завдання (Task Prompt Summary)

1. Причина звернення

Поясніть, чому вас зацікавила ця вакансія помічника в таборі.

2. Попередній досвід

Опишіть свій досвід роботи з дітьми 8–12 років чи допомоги на подіях.

3. Ваша відповідність

Аргументуйте, чому ви гарний кандидат (якісні риси, вміння працювати в команді).

* Обсяг листа: не менше 100 слів. Формат: офіційний/напівофіційний лист адресований Angela Jones.

Корисні фрази та лексика (Useful Phrases)

1. Початок листа та вираження зацікавленості

  • Dear Ms. Jones, Шановна пані Джонс, (стандартне звернення)
  • I am writing to apply for the position of... Я пишу, щоб подати заявку на посаду...
  • I am currently studying on a student exchange programme in Wales. Наразі я навчаюся за програмою студентського обміну в Уельсі.
  • I was delighted to see your advertisement for... Я був/була радий(-а) побачити вашу оголошення про...

2. Опис попереднього досвіду

  • I have relevant experience in... / looking after children. Я маю відповідний досвід у... / догляді за дітьми.
  • In my previous role, I was responsible for organizing games. На моїй попередній посаді я відповідав(-ла) за організацію ігор.
  • I have volunteered at summer camps / school events. Я працював(-ла) волонтером у літніх таборах / школі.

3. Особисті якості та чому саме ви

  • I consider myself to be calm, punctual, and reliable. Я вважаю себе спокійною, пунктуальною та надійною людиною.
  • I possess excellent communication skills and I am a strong team player. Я маю чудові комунікативні навички та вмію працювати в команді.
  • I am passionate about ensuring children’s health and safety. Я з відповідальністю ставлюся до здоров'я та безпеки дітей.

4. Завершення листа

  • I look forward to hearing from you soon. З нетерпінням чекаю вашої відповіді.
  • Yours sincerely, / Yours faithfully, З повагою, (щиро ваш)

Шаблон листа (Template)

Заповніть власні дані у квадратних дужках або скопіюйте текст.

Dear Ms. Jones,

I am writing to apply for the part-time assistant position at Day Camp Global in Cardiff Bay, as advertised recently. I am currently studying on a student exchange programme in Wales, and I am very enthusiastic about this opportunity.

Regarding my experience, I have previously worked as a [ваша попередня роль: e.g. babysitter / volunteer / sports helper], where I was responsible for [основні обов'язки: e.g. looking after children aged 8-12 / organizing activities]. This experience helped me learn how to keep children engaged while strictly maintaining their safety.

I believe I am an ideal candidate for this position because I am [2-3 ваші риси: e.g. a calm, punctual, and highly responsible worker]. Furthermore, I have excellent communication skills, and I am a great team player who loves working with professional trainers and welcoming kids and parents.

I would be delighted to contribute to your camp this summer. Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Yours sincerely,
[Ваше Ім'я та Прізвище]

4 Готові зразки листів (Sample Letters)

Оберіть варіант залежно від вашого профілю чи зацікавленості.

Focus: Babysitting & Childcare Experience | Word Count: ~145 words

Dear Ms. Jones,

I am writing to express my strong interest in the part-time assistant vacancy at Day Camp Global in Cardiff Bay. As an exchange student currently studying in Wales, I am keen to spend my summer helping young learners in a fun and supportive environment.

I have considerable experience looking after children aged eight to twelve. Over the past two years, I worked regularly as a babysitter and assisted at weekend children’s events. My duties included planning engaging indoor games, supervising meal times, and ensuring that all children felt safe and included.

I consider myself to be a calm, punctual, and reliable person who genuinely enjoys working with kids. Furthermore, I am a great team player with strong communication skills, ready to support professional trainers and welcome parents warmly.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Yours sincerely,
Anna Shevchenko

Focus: Sports & Outdoor Games | Word Count: ~150 words

Dear Ms. Jones,

I am writing to apply for the position of part-time assistant at Day Camp Global in Cardiff Bay, as advertised recently. I am currently living in Wales on a student exchange programme and would love to contribute to your team.

I have spent two seasons as a volunteer assistant for a local youth sports club. In that role, I helped coaches organize active outdoor games, managed sports equipment, and ensured children aged 8–12 followed safety rules during training. I am also comfortable handing out leaflets and greeting parents at reception.

I am an energetic, responsible, and fun-loving individual who works exceptionally well in a team. My experience allows me to assist professional trainers effectively while maintaining a high standard of health and safety.

I would be delighted to discuss my application further. I look forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,
Maxym Kovalenko

Focus: Arts, Crafts & Creative Activities | Word Count: ~140 words

Dear Ms. Jones,

I am writing to express my enthusiasm for the part-time assistant position at Day Camp Global, Cardiff Bay. As an exchange student in Wales, I am looking for an exciting opportunity to work with children during the summer.

Last year, I volunteered at a creative art center where I organized drawing, craft workshops, and storytelling sessions for children aged 8 to 11. I am experienced in keeping kids entertained, helping during meal times, and supporting lead instructors during large group projects.

I am a creative, patient, and punctual worker who places high value on safety and team collaboration. I believe my communication skills and positive attitude make me an excellent candidate for this role.

Thank you for considering my application. I hope to have the opportunity to interview with you soon.

Yours sincerely,
Olena Boyko

I am a highly responsible, calm, and well-organized person who communicates effectively. I understand the importance of supervising children's safety and working collaboratively with professional trainers.
